Popular Brewing Techniques

Each brewing method highlights different notes in the coffee bean, from bright acidity to rich chocolate body.

1. Pour Over (V60)

Produces a clean, tea-like cup that highlights floral notes.

2. Espresso

Concentrated coffee brewed under high pressure with rich crema.

3. French Press

Full immersion brewing that yields a heavy, rich body.

4. Cold Brew

Steeped in cold water for 16 hours for a smooth, sweet taste.

5. Moka Pot

Stovetop espresso maker beloved in traditional Italian homes.
